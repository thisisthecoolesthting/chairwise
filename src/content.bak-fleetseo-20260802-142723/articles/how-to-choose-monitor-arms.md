You’ve optimized your chair, perfected your desk height, and maybe even invested in a standing desk converter. But if your monitor is still perched on its factory stand, you’re missing a critical piece of the ergonomic puzzle. A monitor arm isn't just about reclaiming desk space; it's about achieving precise monitor placement, a non-negotiable for anyone spending 8-10 hours in front of a screen. Incorrect monitor height, distance, or angle directly contributes to forward head posture, rounded shoulders, and the associated neck and upper back pain I saw countless times in my occupational therapy practice. This isn't a luxury item for a fully optimized setup; it's a foundational component for healthy computing.
