The wrong chair isn't just uncomfortable; it's a slow, persistent drain on your health and productivity. If you're spending 8-10 hours a day tethered to a screen, the minor aches and stiffness you're feeling now are likely precursors to more significant issues. This guide cuts through the marketing noise to give you a practical framework for selecting an ergonomic office chair that genuinely supports your body and work style.
