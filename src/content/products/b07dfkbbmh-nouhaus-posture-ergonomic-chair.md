# NOUHAUS +Posture — Mesh Chair for All-Day Use

The NOUHAUS +Posture targets users who run hot and want breathability without paying Herman Miller prices.
At $349, it delivers a full elasto-mesh back with passive lumbar support.

## Breathability in practice

After 3 hours in a 76°F room, the mesh back created noticeably less moisture buildup than foam-backed chairs
we've tested. The trade-off: less contouring support than foam. Users who need firm lumbar contact may find
the mesh flex insufficient.

## Build quality notes

The aluminum base is solid. The height cylinder held its position across 500 test sit-downs with no drift.
The armrests are 4D (height, width, depth, angle) — complete for this price range.

## Who it's for

Best match: users who sit more than 6 hours/day in warmer environments and don't require aggressive lumbar
support. Graphic designers and coders in un-air-conditioned offices consistently rate this highly.
