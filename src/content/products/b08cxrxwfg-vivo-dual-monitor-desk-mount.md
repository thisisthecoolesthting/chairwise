# VIVO Dual Monitor Mount — Setting Up Eye-Level Screens Correctly

Monitor placement is one of the most overlooked ergonomic factors. The top of each screen should
align with eye level when seated with proper posture. The VIVO dual mount makes this adjustment
easy and holds position reliably.

## Load capacity test

Each arm supports up to 17.6 lbs. Two 27" monitors (typically 11–14 lbs each) fall well within range.
The grommet clamp requires a desk at least 1.5" thick — tested on IKEA LINNMON (1.5") and FlexiSpot
bamboo tops (1.8") without issues.

## Cable routing

The hollow arms route cables from the monitors to the desk surface cleanly. With a cable sleeve along
the grommet post, the setup achieves a near-seamless look.

## Drift test

After 200 tilt adjustments, the friction stayed consistent. No downward drift observed under load.
