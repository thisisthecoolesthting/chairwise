# FlexiSpot E7 Electric Standing Desk — Stability Test

Standing desks under $500 typically wobble. The E7 is the exception. Dual-motor lifting and a steel frame
cross-brace hold the desktop stable at 49" height with a 28-lb monitor setup on top.

## Wobble measurement

At full standing height (49") with a 27" monitor, keyboard, and small speaker, lateral wobble measured
under 3mm — acceptable for typing, camera work, and standing calls. The cheapest Uplift configurations
wobble 6–8mm at the same height.

## Programming the memory presets

The E7 includes 4 height memory slots. Set slot 1 for seated (28"), slot 2 for standing (43–45" for
most users). Anti-collision stops the motor if it hits an obstacle while lowering.

## Cable management

The included cable tray mounts under the desk. At 21", it's long enough for a 4-outlet power strip and
most monitor cables. The grommets are positioned well for monitor arms.
