## Replacing Your Armrest Caps on a Classic Aeron Chair

If you own a classic Aeron chair, you know its reputation for durability and ergonomic support. However, after years of use, certain components, like the armrest pads, can show wear. Cracked, flattened, or sticky armrest caps can detract from comfort and the chair's overall appearance. Fortunately, replacing these parts is a common and relatively straightforward maintenance task you can perform yourself.

This guide focuses on replacing the armrest pads on a *classic* Aeron chair. It's important to distinguish this from the newer Aeron Remastered chair, as the armrest attachment mechanisms differ.

### Why Replace Armrest Caps?

Beyond aesthetics, worn armrest caps can impact your ergonomic setup. Proper arm support helps alleviate strain on your shoulders and neck. If your current pads are compressed or uneven, they may not be providing adequate support, potentially contributing to discomfort during long work sessions. Replacing them can restore both the comfort and the professional look of your chair.
