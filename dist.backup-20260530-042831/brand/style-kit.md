# chairwise Style Kit

## Brand Summary
chairwise is a clean, evidence-led affiliate review site for ergonomic and home-office chairs. The system balances practical testing credibility with quiet design-magazine restraint.

**Tagline:** Ergonomic chair reviews without the hype.

## Color Palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| primary | #2B2F36 |
| secondary | #5C7A99 |
| accent | #A7B7C8 |
| background | #F2EEE7 |
| text | #25282E |
| muted | #69707A |

Body-text contrast: `#25282E` on `#F2EEE7` = **12.77:1**.

## Type Pairing Recommendation
Wordmark/display: Inter, Carlito, or a humanist sans. UI/body: Inter or Roboto with strong tables and clear callouts.

## Voice & Tone Notes
Plainspoken, evidence-led, and practical. Use phrases like “we measured” and “after six weeks,” and skip inflated claims.

## Do
- Use cool neutrals, simple line geometry, and roomy layouts.
- Lead with fit, support, measurements, and long-term comfort.
- Keep comparisons blunt and useful.

## Don't
- Do not use gamer-neon styling or generic office stock energy.
- Do not overpromise pain fixes.
- Do not bury the buying recommendation under hype.
